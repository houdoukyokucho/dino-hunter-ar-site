Privacy Policy

App: Dino Hunter AR
Developer: Tomoyuki Suzuki
Effective date: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tomoyuki Suzuki ("the developer") handles information in the iOS app "Dino Hunter AR" ("the app").

1. Information we collect

The app does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or contacts. The app has no account registration.

2. Camera

The app uses your device's camera to display dinosaurs in AR (augmented reality). The camera feed is used only on your device to render the AR view. The app does not save or transmit any camera images or video.

The app asks for camera permission the first time it is needed. If you do not allow it, the AR features will not work. You can change this permission at any time in the Settings app.

3. Data storage and transmission

The app does not store your data, including game scores, on your device or on any server. The app does not send your information to the developer or to any third party.

The app is built with the Unity game engine. The Unity engine may send non-personal technical information (such as device model, OS version, and error details) to Unity Technologies for diagnostics.

4. Children

The app is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

5. Changes to this policy

The developer may update this policy as needed. Any changes will be posted on this page.
