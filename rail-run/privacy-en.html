Privacy Policy

App: Rail Run
Effective date: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the iOS app "Rail Run" ("the app").

1. Information the app collects

The app does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or contacts. The app has no account registration or login.

Your best score is stored only on your device and is never sent to the developer or anyone else.

2. Advertising

The app shows ads using Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC. To serve ads, measure their performance, and prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect information such as:

  • Device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow it)
  • Approximate location estimated from your IP address
  • Usage data about the app and ads, such as ad views and taps
  • Diagnostic data about app performance and crashes

This information is handled by Google. For details, please see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

3. Tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency)

When you first open the app, it asks for permission to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. You can use every feature of the app without allowing it. If you allow it, ads may be more relevant to you.

You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

4. Children

The app is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

5. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as needed. Any changes will be posted on this page.
