Rail Run Support

Rail Run is an endless runner where you trace with your finger to lay rails in front of a train that never stops. If the track runs out, or you hit a town or an animal, the train derails.

Requirements

  • iOS 15.0 or later
  • iPhone

How to play

  • Trace with your finger from the glowing yellow end of the track to extend it.
  • Traced too far? Trace back along the track to undo it.
  • Your score is the distance traveled. The train gets faster as you go.

FAQ

I can't lay track

Start tracing near the glowing yellow end of the track. You can't lay track on buildings or on the water behind the starting line.

Why do I see ads?

The app is free to play and shows a full-screen ad once every few game overs. Ads never appear while you are playing.

The app asked for tracking permission

This permission is used to show ads that are more relevant to you. Every feature works without it. You can change it later in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Is my best score saved?

Yes, on your device. It is removed if you delete the app.

Do you collect personal information?

The app itself does not. For information collected by Google AdMob to show ads, please see the Privacy Policy.
