Dino Hunter AR Support

Dino Hunter AR is an AR shooting game where dinosaurs appear on your floor in AR and you shoot them down.

Requirements

  • iOS 15.0 or later
  • An ARKit-compatible iPhone or iPad
  • Camera permission

Play safely

You move around while looking at the screen. Play in a large, safe space and watch out for people, objects, steps, and furniture. Do not use the app while walking on streets or in other dangerous places.

FAQ

No dinosaurs appear

Point the camera at the floor and move your device slowly until the app detects the floor. Dark rooms, plain white floors, and places with many mirrors or windows can make the floor hard to detect.

The screen is black and the camera view does not show

Camera access may not be allowed. Go to Settings > Dino Hunter AR > Camera, turn it on, and restart the app.

Are scores saved?

Scores are not saved in the current version.

Do you collect personal information?

No. See the Privacy Policy for details.
